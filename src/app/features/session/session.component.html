Temporary by design

In memory, not in storage

  • The session expires after 30 minutes. Reloading or logging out ends it sooner.
  • The app does not save credentials or tokens to browser storage. Tokens are never displayed.
  • Your password is cleared after success, failure or cancellation. Browser autofill is discouraged but may ignore this page’s settings.
  • Requests time out after 15 seconds and never retry automatically. Leaving cancels pending work.
  • Logging out keeps your anonymous cart. Reloading clears the in-memory cart too.
