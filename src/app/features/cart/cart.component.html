What is saved?

Only this browser app session. Reload clears the cart; route navigation does not. The first add captures the price and stock, and later adds keep that snapshot. Inventory is not reserved or refreshed. Remove and re-add to capture a new snapshot. Up to 99 per product and 100 different products; actual snapshot stock may be lower.

One private signal owns the lines. Item count and subtotal are computed; quantity controls emit intent, not direct mutations.
